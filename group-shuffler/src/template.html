How the group shuffler works

Use Student numbers and enter your class size to group students by number. If you want names or initials on the groups, choose Paste labels and enter one label per line.

Set the number of groups you need and select Shuffle groups. Everyone gets assigned to one group, and group sizes differ by at most one student. For example, 26 students in six groups gives you two groups of five and four groups of four.

Move or swap students

Select one student. To move them, choose Move here in the destination group. To swap, select a student in another group and the two students will trade places. Use Undo move or Undo swap to reverse the last change.

Share the finished groups

Use Copy groups to paste the assignments into a slide or document. Print groups prints just the group lists and their headings. The roster stays in the open tab, so copy or print the groups before closing it.

To use the tool without internet, download the shuffler and open the HTML file in your browser. You can also view the source code on GitHub.
